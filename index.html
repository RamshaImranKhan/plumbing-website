    Plumbing FAQs

    Common questions from Omaha homeowners. Need help now? Call us 24/7.

    Do you offer emergency plumbing service in Omaha?

    Yes — we are available 24/7 to take your call for emergencies. Our team serves Omaha, Papillion, Council Bluffs, and surrounding areas around the clock.

    How often should I schedule drain cleaning?

    For most Omaha homes, professional drain cleaning once a year helps prevent clogs, odors, and costly backups. Older pipes or large trees nearby may require more frequent service.

    What maintenance should I do for my water heater?

    Flush the tank annually to clear sediment, check the pressure relief valve, inspect for rust or leaks, and keep the thermostat around 120°F. We offer professional inspections and $59 flushes.

    Do you offer plumbing financing?

    We've built relationships with several lenders so customers can restore functionality in their homes. Ask your technician about financing options during your visit.

    How do I prepare my plumbing for winter in Omaha?

    Insulate exposed pipes in unheated areas, disconnect outdoor hoses, shut off exterior water lines, and know where your main shut-off valve is in case of a frozen pipe emergency.

    How much does a plumber cost?

    Prices vary depending on the type of service and labor required. We provide upfront cost estimations with your budget in mind — no surprise fees after the job is done.

    When is a plumbing permit required?

    Most plumbing services require a permit. Permits are usually needed for new installations, piping replacement, drain line replacement, water heater replacement or upgrades, relocating plumbing, and any sewer line work.

    Does a plumber do gas lines?

    Yes! Our experts provide gas line repair, replacement, and installation. If you are experiencing a gas leak, call your gas company immediately — they will secure the area before we perform repairs.
