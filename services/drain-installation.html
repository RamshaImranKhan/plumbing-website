When Is It Time to Replace My Drain?

While we'll always default to repairing drains and sewer lines if that solution meets your needs, there does come a day when a drain will need to be replaced. Other common signs that it's time to install a new drain:

  • Your home is 50 years old or older (or you don't know when the drains were last replaced)
  • You've had the same drain problems repeatedly for several months or years
  • Your water fixtures are backing up with water from another fixture on the same floor
  • You're seeing clogs despite not flushing anything harmful down the drain
  • Your property has or is near a large tree (will require inspection)
