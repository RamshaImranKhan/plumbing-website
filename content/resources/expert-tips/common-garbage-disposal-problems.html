Common Garbage Disposal Problems

Common Garbage Disposal Problems

Once you have a garbage disposal, it's hard to imagine living without one. These convenient kitchen appliances transform drains into food disposal systems.

A malfunctioning unit will cause food disposal problems, so it's important to understand how to troubleshoot common issues.

Regular Garbage Disposal Troubles

If you have any of these garbage disposal problems, you're not alone. You can sometimes address these common issues with DIY tips and tricks or with the help of professional plumbers.

Not Switching On

There are many possible reasons your garbage disposal won't switch on. First, check if it's plugged in and then double-check your circuit breaker for tripped switches. If power isn't the issue, turn off the disposal, press the reset switch, and try it again. If that doesn't work, consider garbage disposal repair services from trusted experts.

Louder Than Normal Noises

Unusual noises can mean there's a foreign object lodged in the blades or that one or more components are worn out. Switch off the unit, carefully inspect it for obstructions, and seek help from professional plumbers if the issue persists.

Humming Without Grinding

A humming noise suggests the motor is running, but the blades have jammed, preventing grinding. There are ways to safely reset your garbage disposal to resolve the problem. Otherwise, call an expert to restore proper function.

Unpleasant Odors

Some garbage disposal trouble can result in foul smells wafting up from your sink. Running citrus peels and ice cubes through the system can help, or you can clean your garbage disposal with hot water and liquid soap.

Leaks

Garbage disposals can leak from various areas. If you'd like to find the source of the leak before calling a plumber, go ahead — you've got this. However, the repair job might be tricky, so be careful and know when to hand it over to an expert.

Slow Drainage or Standing Water

Slow drainage means your garbage disposal isn't working the way it should and needs cleaning. If water is standing, there is a blockage that needs to be unclogged.

Clogs

If you do have a partial clog or complete blockage, you can try to unclog your garbage disposal yourself or choose to consult a professional plumber for safety and efficiency. Remember, always throw foods that are unsuitable for garbage disposal in the trash.
