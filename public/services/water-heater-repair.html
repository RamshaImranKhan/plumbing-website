Signs of a Failing Water Heater

  • Reduced amount of hot water
  • Leaking water
  • Randomly shutting off
  • Standing water in the unit
  • Running constantly
  • Increasing energy bills

Common Causes of Hot Water Heater Problems

  • Sediment buildup
  • Faulty temperature and pressure relief valve
  • Defective drain valve
  • Damaged tank
  • Sulfate bacteria buildup
  • Deteriorating anode rod
  • Loose pipe connections

Identifying Water Heater Sounds

  • Popping — likely caused by hot water bubbling up through sediment buildup at the bottom of the tank.
  • Crackling — likely caused by calcium exploding inside a tank.
  • Ticking — likely caused by fluctuations in water pressure or loose pipe straps.
  • Sizzling — likely caused by water leaking onto a hot burner assembly.
  • Hammering/knocking — likely caused by an abruptly halted water flow inside supply pipes.

From tightening pipe straps to replacing pressure valves, we have expert remedies for every situation.

Water Heater Maintenance

Your water heater should be inspected by a licensed plumber every year. Our maintenance services include:

  • Routine hot water heater flushing
  • Part replacements
  • Multi-point inspections for cracks, leaks, and joint gaps
  • Anode rod examinations
  • Temperature and pressure valve safety checks

Benefits of Water Heater Maintenance

  • Improved performance — including insulation recommendations for hot tanks.
  • Increased efficiency — removing sediment, fixing leaks, and calibrating temperature.
  • Better longevity — expansion tanks and water softening systems to mitigate hard water.
  • Reduced energy bills — keeping your tank in prime condition extends its lifespan.

Water Heater Repair FAQs

Should I repair or replace my water heater?

When the cost of repairing a water heater is 50% or more of the cost of replacing it, we almost always recommend installing a new unit. Upgrading can provide unlimited hot water, compact sizes, reduced energy usage, and increased reliability.

What do water heater repairs cost?

Repair costs involve the specific part(s) being fixed, the make and model, the condition of the unit, and the labor and time required. We provide straightforward pricing before work begins.

Should I shower if my water heater is leaking?

No. Not repairing a leak immediately can result in significant damage — breaker trips, mold, water damage, and fire hazards. A leak can also indicate potentially dangerous structural issues.

How can I detect if my water heater is leaking?

The best solution is installing a leak alert and automatic shut-off system. This technology connects a valve to the water line feed and a sensor near the drain tube to minimize damage.

Can water heaters catch fire?

In rare, extreme cases, a water heater could explode and cause a fire due to a gas leak near the pilot light, or excessive water pressure when safety mechanisms fail.

How often should I drain my water heater?

We recommend having your water heater flushed at least once a year to prevent sediment buildup and extend the life of your unit.
